# Spec (lite) — Chapter 3: the escaped farm animals (roadmap step 23)

> Data: `content/chapters/chapter3_animals.tres`; played after Chapter 2, then the practice loop.

## 5. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01 The farmer asks for help.** By the barn the farmer (his own English voice) waves:
  "Hello! Oh no! My animals ran away! The cow, the sheep, the duck and the pig!" … "Can you find
  them and bring them home?"; he says hello back.
- **AC-02 Find each animal.** One at a time: "Where is the cow? Find the cow!" — the cow by the
  harbour, the sheep in the park, the duck by the sea, the pig on the square. Each animal is a
  toy-like model that softly glows. Clicking it: "Moo! Moo! The cow says moo! Cow! Go home,
  cow!", and it trots back to the farm and stays there. First meetings open the learning card.
- **AC-03 All home.** "All the animals are home!"; the farmer thanks him, a card for *farm*,
  "Hooray! Moo, baa, quack, oink!". Remembered (`chapter:animals`).
