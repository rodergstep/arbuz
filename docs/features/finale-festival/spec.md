# Spec (lite) — Finale: the festival (roadmap step 25)

> Data: `content/chapters/chapter5_festival.tres`; played after Chapter 4, then the practice loop.

## 5. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01 The square gets ready.** When the clock works again, the square is dressed for the
  party: flag garlands from the clock tower, balloon bunches, a table with a big cake, the farm
  animals. Every friend he helped (the postman, the baker, the toy maker, the farmer, the clock
  maker) walks in and stands round the square. Tygrytsa: "Listen! Ding dong! The party is
  starting! Let's go to the square!"
- **AC-02 Every friend says thank you.** At the square each friend in turn faces him, waves and
  thanks him in their own voice, naming what he did (the letter, the cake, the balloons, the
  animals, the clock).
- **AC-03 He says hello back.** "Everybody says hello! Now you! Say: hello!" (microphone,
  never judged); then a learning card for *party*.
- **AC-04 Hooray.** Everybody waves, a gentle burst of confetti and a short tune, "Hooray! It's
  a party! Thank you, friends!". Remembered (`chapter:festival`); then the practice loop. From
  then on the town stays in festival dress with every friend on the square.

## Out of scope

Music track, dancing animations, the friends gallery (step 20).
