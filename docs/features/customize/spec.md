# Spec (lite) — Customization: paint shop and hats (roadmap step 27)

## 5. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01 Paint shop.** Near the car in each town stand four big paint pots: red (dots), blue
  (stripes), yellow (a plus), green (a ring): colour is never the only cue. Clicking one repaints
  his car (every shade of its body colour) and says "Blue! Now our car is blue!"; the colour word's
  card teaches it the first time.
- **AC-02 Hat stand.** Beside it, a cap, a sun hat and a crown on little stands. Clicking one puts
  it on the boy's head (new words: cap, hat).
- **AC-03 Kept.** His car colour and hat are kept in the save settings (`car_color`, `hat`).
