# Spec (lite) — The album: friends and treasures (roadmap step 20, part 1)

## 5. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01 Always there.** A big book button in the top-right corner of both towns opens his album
  ("Look! Your friends and your treasures!"); a big ✕ closes it. The game waits behind it.
- **AC-02 Friends he helped.** The postman (after the prologue), the baker, the toy maker, the
  farmer and the clock maker (after their chapters) appear as live, slowly turning 3D pictures.
- **AC-03 Treasures he found.** The six hidden treasures appear once found (`treasure:<id>`).
- **AC-04 Souvenirs replay their word.** Tapping a picture says "This is a teddy." (English).
  Ones not found yet show a soft "?".

Out of scope (later): the world map that unlocks, a parent progress view.
