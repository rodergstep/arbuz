# Spec (lite) — Chapter 2: the toy maker's balloons (roadmap step 22)

> Data: `content/chapters/chapter2_balloons.tres`; played after Chapter 1, then the practice loop.

## 5. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01 The toy maker asks for help.** By the toy shop the toy maker (her own English voice)
  waves: "Hello! Oh dear! My balloons flew away! Red, blue, yellow and green!" … "Can you find my
  balloons? Please!"; Tygrytsa explains (Ukrainian the first time); he says hello back.
- **AC-02 Find each colour.** One balloon at a time: "Find the red balloon!" (park), blue
  (harbour), yellow (square), green (by the trees). Each balloon has its colour **and its own
  pattern** (red dots, blue stripes, yellow plus signs, green upright rings), so colour is never
  the only cue. Clicking it: "A red balloon! You found it! Red!"; the first time a colour opens its
  learning card. Tygrytsa points at it and hints.
- **AC-03 Thanks.** Back at the toy shop: "Thank you! Red, blue, yellow and green! My
  balloons!", a learning card for *balloon*, "Hooray! Balloons!". Remembered (`chapter:balloons`).
- Before this chapter the parents may want the colour-vision check from the development plan;
  the patterns keep the chapter playable either way.
