# Spec (lite) — Chapter 1: the bakery (roadmap step 19)

## 5. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01 The baker asks for help.** By her bakery, the baker (her own English voice) waves:
  "Hello! Oh no, oh no! I lost my apples, my eggs, my milk and my flour!" … "Can you help me?";
  Tygrytsa explains (Ukrainian the first time) and asks him to say hello back (microphone,
  never judged).
- **AC-02 Find and count.** Tygrytsa: "Find the apples! Let's count them!". Three apples (park),
  two eggs (farm), the milk (café) and the flour (bus stop) glow softly where they lie and hop.
  Each click collects one and counts aloud: "One apple!", "Two apples!", "Three apples!". The
  first of each kind opens its learning card if he hasn't met the word (teach before test).
  Tygrytsa points at the nearest one and hints after ~15 s.
- **AC-03 Back to the bakery.** "Let's bring everything to the baker!"; the bakery's password
  door asks one of the words he has learned (apple, egg, milk, flour, …).
- **AC-04 The cake.** The baker: "Thank you! … Now I can bake my cake!", a learning card for
  *cake*, "Hooray! A cake! Yummy!". Remembered in the save (`chapter:bakery`); then the practice
  loop.
- The bakery door only asks when the quest is at it (never while he passes by on another task).
