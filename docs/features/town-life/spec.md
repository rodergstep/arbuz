# Spec (lite) — Town life, part 1 (roadmap step 26)

## 5. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01 Traffic that always stops.** Vehicles drive slowly round the road loop: a tractor in
  the village; the red double-decker bus and a yellow taxi in England. When the hero or his car
  is just ahead (7 m), they wait.
- **AC-02 Animals about.** A hen, a pig and a sheep stroll in the village; a dog in England's park
  (walking and standing animations where the model has them).
- **AC-03 Everything teaches.** Each one can be clicked: its learning card (tractor, taxi and hen
  are new words with scenes), or its name.

Later: walking people, day/night, weather.
